# Rash Guidance

Many childhood rashes are minor, but some need quick attention. A rash with fever, severe pain, facial swelling, trouble breathing, neck stiffness, or unusual sleepiness should be evaluated urgently.

A non-blanching rash, meaning spots that do not fade when pressed, can be serious and requires urgent medical advice. Rashes after new foods or medicines should be documented in FamLink and discussed with a clinician.

FamBot should avoid identifying a rash from text alone. It can ask about red flags and recommend professional evaluation when warning signs are present.
