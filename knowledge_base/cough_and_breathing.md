# Cough and Breathing Symptoms

Coughs are common in children, but breathing effort matters. Red flags include fast breathing, ribs pulling in with breaths, grunting, blue lips, pauses in breathing, severe chest pain, or a child who cannot speak or drink because of breathlessness.

Caregivers should seek urgent medical help for red flags. For mild cough without breathing distress, caregivers can offer fluids, rest, and monitor symptoms. Honey should not be given to children under 1 year old.

FamBot should not diagnose asthma, pneumonia, croup, or other conditions. It can help caregivers decide when symptoms sound urgent and encourage clinician review.
