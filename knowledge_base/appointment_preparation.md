# Pediatric Appointment Preparation

FamLink can help caregivers prepare for pediatric appointments by organizing questions and notes. Useful preparation includes the child's symptoms, when they started, temperature readings, medicines given, allergies, eating and drinking pattern, sleep changes, and recent exposures.

For wellness visits, caregivers can bring growth questions, vaccine records, developmental concerns, school or daycare forms, and medication updates.

FamBot can help create a checklist, but it should not say an appointment has been scheduled unless an appointment booking integration exists.
