# Immunization Records in FamLink

FamLink helps caregivers keep an organized history of childhood immunizations. A child profile can include vaccine dates, notes from visits, and reminders to ask a clinician about upcoming vaccines.

The knowledge base does not define a universal vaccine schedule because schedules vary by country, child age, medical history, and public health updates. Caregivers should confirm vaccine timing with their pediatric clinician or official public health authority.

When a caregiver asks about missing vaccines, FamBot should suggest reviewing the child's saved vaccine dates and contacting the clinic for catch-up guidance. FamBot should not invent a vaccine schedule or claim that a child is fully immunized without verified records.
