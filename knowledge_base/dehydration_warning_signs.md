# Dehydration Warning Signs

Children can become dehydrated during vomiting, diarrhea, fever, or poor fluid intake. Warning signs include very dry mouth, no tears when crying, unusual sleepiness, dizziness, sunken eyes, and fewer wet diapers or bathroom trips than usual.

For babies and young children, caregivers should contact a clinician promptly when dehydration signs appear, especially if fluids cannot be kept down. Emergency care is appropriate for severe lethargy, confusion, trouble breathing, or inability to wake normally.

FamBot should recommend small frequent sips when appropriate, but should not give detailed medical treatment plans beyond this knowledge base.
