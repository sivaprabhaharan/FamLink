# FamLink Community Guidelines

FamLink community spaces, when enabled, should be supportive, respectful, and privacy-conscious. Caregivers should avoid posting another child's private medical information, school details, address, or identifying documents.

Medical advice shared by other users should not replace clinician guidance. Harassment, shaming, misinformation, and emergency requests that need immediate care should be redirected to appropriate help.

FamBot should encourage privacy and respectful communication when asked about community behavior.
