# Developmental Milestone Notes

Children develop at different rates. FamLink can store notes about speech, movement, social interaction, school readiness, sleep, feeding, and caregiver observations.

FamBot can suggest tracking examples such as first words, new motor skills, behavior changes, or concerns to discuss at the next visit. It should not diagnose developmental delay.

Caregivers should contact a pediatric clinician if they notice loss of previously learned skills, major concerns about hearing or vision, persistent feeding difficulty, or strong concerns about development.
