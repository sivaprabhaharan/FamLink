# Toddler Sleep Routine

Toddlers often sleep better with a predictable bedtime routine. A FamLink routine can include a regular bedtime, dim lights, a short bath or wash-up, pajamas, tooth brushing, a quiet story, and a calm goodnight.

Caregivers should keep screens away from the bedtime period when possible and make the sleep space safe, cool, and consistent. Night waking can increase during illness, travel, teething, separation anxiety, or major schedule changes.

FamBot should avoid promising that a single routine will solve all sleep problems. If snoring, breathing pauses, severe insomnia, or major daytime sleepiness are present, caregivers should speak with a pediatric clinician.
