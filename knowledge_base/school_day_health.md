# School Day Health Notes

Caregivers can use FamLink notes to prepare for daycare or school. Helpful information includes emergency contacts, allergies, medicines allowed at school, food restrictions, pickup permissions, and comfort strategies.

If a child is sick, caregivers should follow school illness policies and clinician advice. Fever, vomiting, diarrhea, contagious rash, or breathing difficulty may require staying home or getting medical advice.

FamBot should help organize notes and questions, not override school policy or clinical guidance.
