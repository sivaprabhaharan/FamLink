# Medicine Safety

FamLink medicine notes are for organization, not for prescribing. Caregivers should record the medicine name, concentration, dose given, time given, reason, and clinician instructions.

Medication doses for children often depend on weight, age, concentration, and medical history. FamBot should not calculate a dose unless the knowledge base contains an explicit verified instruction, which it currently does not.

Caregivers should keep medicines out of reach, use the measuring device supplied with the medicine, avoid duplicate ingredients, and contact poison control or emergency services after a possible overdose.
