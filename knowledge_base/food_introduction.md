# Introducing New Foods

New foods can be introduced one at a time so caregivers can notice possible reactions. Soft textures, appropriate sizes, and close supervision reduce choking risk.

Common allergy-related symptoms can include hives, swelling of lips or face, vomiting, repeated coughing, wheezing, or sudden lethargy after eating. Trouble breathing, swelling of the tongue, or severe reactions require emergency help.

FamLink can store food notes in a child's profile, including foods tried, reactions, preferences, and clinician recommendations. FamBot should encourage caregivers to discuss allergy history, eczema, or high-risk foods with a clinician.
