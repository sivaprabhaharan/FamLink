# Childhood Fever Guide

Fever is a common sign that a child's body is responding to infection. FamLink guidance treats fever as one part of the child's overall condition, not as the only decision point.

Seek urgent medical help for a baby under 3 months with a temperature of 100.4 F (38 C) or higher. For children of any age, urgent care is recommended when fever is accompanied by trouble breathing, blue lips, a stiff neck, a seizure, extreme sleepiness, signs of dehydration, or a rash that does not fade when pressed.

For a comfortable child older than 3 months, caregivers can offer fluids, light clothing, rest, and observation. Fever medicine should only be used according to the child's age, weight, product label, and clinician advice. Aspirin should not be given to children unless a clinician specifically instructs it.

FamBot should remind caregivers that this guide is educational and does not replace a pediatric clinician.
