# Privacy and Family Data

FamLink stores family-related details such as child profiles, allergies, notes, and care routines. Caregivers should only enter information they are comfortable storing in the app environment they control.

FamBot responses may be traced for observability when the backend is enabled. Tracing should capture useful debugging details such as the user question, retrieved sources, model name, and scores, while avoiding unnecessary secrets.

Caregivers should avoid entering passwords, government ID numbers, or highly sensitive information into chat unless the app explicitly supports secure handling for that data.
